Mlyn Aidoku Sources

Curated personal Aidoku source list focused strictly on the 17 extensions I actively read (Atsumaru, Comix, Kagane, MANGA Plus, Mangaball, MangaBat, MangaDex, MangaDotNet, MangaFire, Mangago, MangaKakalot, MangaNato, MangaNelo, MangaPill, Toonily, Weeb Central), ported from Mihon (keiyoushi/extensions-source) and Aidoku upstream with automatic upstream sync.

Requires Aidoku 0.7 or newer (0.8.4+ for Comix / MangaDotNet v20, 0.9.0+ for Mangadotnet v10).

You can add this repository inside the Aidoku app in the Settings tab under Source Lists by entering: index.min.json
